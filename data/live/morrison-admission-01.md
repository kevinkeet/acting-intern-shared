## Scoring

- **must**: Critical: must be said unprompted in every mode, within window_s of the cue line ending. One alert may cover two items (E7+E8).
- **lead**: Important: expected in Lead mode; in Follow/Abreast it may be held (not a miss). ok_in_any_mode means saying it is never a false alarm.
- **answers**: Each question must get a correct answer; judged against the summary (keywords are a quick pre-check).
- **false_alarm**: In Follow and Abreast, any unprompted remark that matches no expectation. In Lead, unmatched remarks are judged as extras (useful or noise).
- **repeat**: Saying the same thing twice counts against it, unless the second adds new, more urgent information (E3 after E1 is fine).
